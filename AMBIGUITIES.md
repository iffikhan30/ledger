# AMBIGUITIES.md — interpretation log

1. **Posted day versus value date.** E7 is listed as posted on Day 5 but has value date Day 2. The implementation keeps both fields: replay order controls when the event is learned; value date controls historical ledger inclusion. When E7 arrives, prior closes through Day 5 are recalculated for fee discovery.
2. **Meaning of “closing balance.”** A close is the sum of opening balance and all ledger entries whose value date is on or before the requested day. Authorization holds are excluded from ledger balance and subtracted only to calculate available balance.
3. **Late overdraft fee behavior.** A fee is appended when a replayed event first reveals a negative historical close. It is booked on that historical value day and assessed once per account/day. Fees already booked are not deleted or automatically reversed by later entries; the brief defines no fee-reversal policy.
4. **Fee-induced balances.** The fee itself is an ordinary signed ledger entry and therefore affects later closing balances. The scan is chronological by value day so an earlier fee can affect later days' fee eligibility.
5. **E7's fee consequences.** Before fee entries, E7 makes Day 2 close -370.00 and, after the Day 3 credit and Day 4 Auth-A settlement, Day 4 close -155.00. Consequently both Day 2 and Day 4 qualify for fees when E7 arrives late. The Day 2 fee then affects Day 3 and later balances.
6. **Authorization timing and later backdating.** Authorizations are decided at their point in event-stream replay using the ledger known at that moment, current active holds, and the authorization's value day. A later backdated debit does not retroactively rewrite an earlier approval. Auth-B is evaluated after E7 and the newly discovered fees, so it is declined.
7. **Auth-A settlement.** An approved active authorization may settle for a positive amount no greater than the original authorization amount. The full hold is released after an accepted settlement; the ledger is debited by the actual settlement amount (185.00), not the hold amount (200.00).
8. **Unknown Auth-Z settlement.** There is no preceding Auth-Z authorization. E6 is rejected and creates no ledger entry.
9. **Reversal semantics.** E9 is a compensating +620.00 entry against E7's -620.00 entry, retaining E7's original value date. Neither original event nor entry is mutated/deleted. The reversal does not automatically reverse fee entries, because no such policy is specified.
10. **E10 order.** E10 is processed at its stated position after E9, even though its posted day is Day 5. The prompt says replay the event stream in this order; it is not sorted by posted day.
11. **Equal BHD instalments.** Exact equality at three decimal places conflicts with the exact total: 10.000 / 3 = 3.333… BHD. We choose 3.333, 3.333, 3.334; the final instalment absorbs the residual. Which instalment absorbs it is not specified, so the final one is chosen for auditability.
12. **Interest after late events.** Interest is calculated after all events have been replayed, from each day's final closing ledger balance before the interest capitalization entry. Each daily accrual is rounded to currency precision; their sum is capitalized as one Day 6 credit. This avoids accruing interest on uncapitalized prior daily interest.
13. **Interest on non-positive balances.** “Positive balances only” is interpreted as zero accrual for zero or negative closing balances.
14. **Currency scope of overdraft fee.** The fee is explicitly AED 25.00, while ACC-002 is BHD. No FX conversion or BHD-equivalent fee is specified, so the AED-denominated fee is applied only to the AED account.
15. **Rounding mode.** The brief requires currency precision but does not specify a tie-breaking mode. `ROUND_HALF_UP` is selected and documented for deterministic results.
16. **“After E9, all balances and fees return to pre-E7 values.”** “Pre-E7” could mean ledger movements only or all accounting consequences. We interpret it as the full ledger state. The debit is compensated, but fee entries remain, so the criterion is false.
17. **Output timing.** The report is a final replay report after interest capitalization. Historical “before fee” or “before interest” figures are explicitly distinguished in tests/docs rather than confused with the final ledger close.
